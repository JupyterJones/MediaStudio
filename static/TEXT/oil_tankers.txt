what is the aprox value of an oil tanker inventory
The approximate value of an oil tanker’s inventory depends on three main things:
Type of tanker (capacity)
Type of oil carried
Current oil price per barrel
I’ll give you realistic ballpark numbers so you can reason about scale without needing live market data.
Typical Oil Tanker Capacities
Tanker Type	Capacity (barrels)
Handysize	250,000 – 500,000
Aframax	500,000 – 800,000
Suezmax	~1,000,000
VLCC (Very Large Crude Carrier)	~2,000,000
ULCC (Ultra Large)	3,000,000 – 4,000,000
Most headlines and geopolitical stories involve VLCCs.
Oil Price Assumption (Rounded)
To keep it simple, let’s assume:
$70–$90 per barrel (typical modern range)
I’ll show numbers using $80/barrel as a midpoint.
Inventory Value by Tanker Type
🛢️ Aframax (~700,000 barrels)
700,000 × $80 ≈ $56 million
🛢️ Suezmax (~1,000,000 barrels)
1,000,000 × $80 ≈ $80 million
🛢️ VLCC (~2,000,000 barrels)
2,000,000 × $80 ≈ $160 million
🛢️ ULCC (4,000,000 barrels)
4,000,000 × $80 ≈ $320 million
Real-World Rule of Thumb
An oil tanker typically carries between $50 million and $300+ million worth of oil.
That’s inventory only, not counting:
The ship itself ($80–$150M new)
Insurance
Charter contracts
Hedging
Futures pricing
Fun (and slightly terrifying) Perspective 😌
A single VLCC tanker can carry:
More value than a large bank branch
Comparable to a major tech company’s data center
Enough energy to power a small country for days
Which is why oil tankers are:
Heavily insured
Constantly tracked
Strategically important in wars and trade routes
